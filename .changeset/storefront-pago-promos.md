Pago integrado y promociones (contrato Storefront API 1.2, aditivo). Tipos: `PaymentOption`, `PaymentClient` (con `csp`), `PaymentAction`/`PaymentVoucher`, `PaymentAttemptResult`, `DeclineCode`, `CartPromotions`, `CartDiscountLine`, `CartRecommendation`, `PromotionAnnouncement`; campos opcionales en `PaymentMethod` (`options`, `client`), `CheckoutInput` (`payment_flow`, `payment_option`, `expected_total`), `CheckoutPayment` (`flow`, `client`, `action`), `Cart.promotions` y `CartCost.discounts`. Cliente: `checkouts.paymentConfig(id)` (`GET /storefront/checkouts/:id/payment-config`) y `checkouts.pay(id, input, { idempotencyKey })` (`POST .../payment`; la llave es obligatoria y lanza antes de llamar), `carts.setNote`, `carts.recommendations`, `promotions.list`. Helpers puros es/en y sin DOM: `paymentOutcome` (approved / in_review / voucher / challenge / redirect / rejected / expired / cancelled; solo https en fichas y redirecciones), `paymentErrorView` (red → reintento con el mismo intento; 409/422/429), `declineMessage` (motivo accionable por `decline_code`), `formatVoucherExpiry`, `paymentIdempotencyKey`, `promoProgressView`, `rewardLabel`, `normalizeNote`. Nada calcula montos: `remaining`, `percent` y `reached` son del servidor. El mock sirve `POST /checkouts/:id/payment` (resultados por token `tok_APRO`, `tok_FUND`, `tok_3DS`..., efectivo con ficha, idempotencia, 409/422/429), `PUT /carts/:id/note`, `GET /carts/:id/recommendations`, `GET /promotions` y `GET /checkouts/:id/payment-config`. Alineado con `docs/PAGO-INTEGRADO.md` del addon `mercadopago` y con `pay.go` de `storefront_cart` (`outcome`, `next_step`, voucher con `amount` + `currency`).
